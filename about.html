Our story

Built by drivers who got tired of unclear fares.

Shreyas Tours and Travels started in 2018 with two sedans and one simple rule: quote the full fare before the trip starts, every time. Seven years on, that rule hasn't changed — only the fleet has grown.

Mission

Make one-day travel decisions boring, in the best way.

Deciding how to get to the airport or plan a family day trip shouldn't involve five phone calls and a fare negotiation. We keep one promise: tell us the trip, get one fixed number back, and know exactly who is driving before they arrive.

How we operate

Local knowledge, professional standards

Every driver is a full-time employee, not a gig contractor — background checked, trained on customer service, and familiar with Mumbai's traffic patterns, festival diversions and monsoon routes.

What guides us

Three commitments we don't bend on

Fare, before the ride

You get a written, fixed quote covering fuel, tolls and driver charges before you confirm — never after.

Verified, not anonymous

Every driver's name, photo and licence details are shared with you at booking, and their trip history is tracked internally.

Someone always answers

Our support line runs 24x7, including festivals and monsoon weekends, because that's exactly when plans change.
