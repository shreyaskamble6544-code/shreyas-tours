10 Best One-Day Trips Near Mumbai for 2026

Mumbai's traffic makes distance deceptive — a place 90 km away can take longer than one 140 km away, depending on the route. Here's a realistic, drive-time-sorted list of getaways you can actually complete in a single day.

Under 2 hours: quick escapes

These routes work even if you're leaving after a late breakfast.

  • Karnala Bird Sanctuary — a short trek to a hilltop fort and reliable birdwatching, good for an early-morning start.
  • Kihim Beach, Alibaug — quieter than the main Alibaug beaches, reachable by road via the Mumbai-Goa highway.
  • Matheran — leave the car at Neral and take the toy train or a shared jeep up; the hill station itself is vehicle-free.

2 to 3 hours: the classic hill-station run

  • Lonavala & Khandala — waterfalls in monsoon, valley viewpoints year-round, and the Karla-Bhaja cave complex nearby.
  • Kamshet — quieter than Lonavala, popular for paragliding on clear days.
  • Igatpuri — Bhandardara-adjacent hills with a large lake and Buddhist meditation centre.

3 to 4 hours: worth the extra distance

  • Nashik — vineyard tours in the morning, Sula and Soma estates both run day visits, plus the Trimbakeshwar temple circuit.
  • Mahabaleshwar — strawberry season (roughly December to March) makes the extra hour worth it; go early to beat weekend traffic.
  • Bhandardara — Wilson Dam and Arthur Lake, less commercial than Lonavala, best on a weekday.

Planning tip: build in a buffer both ways

Every route above assumes normal traffic. On long weekends, add 60–90 minutes to your return leg specifically — the bottleneck is usually the exit points back into Mumbai (Sion, Vashi and the Western Express Highway), not the highway itself.
