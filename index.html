Frequently asked questions

How do I book a local day tour or cab?

Call or WhatsApp us at +91 7588291375, or fill in the form on our Contact page with your pickup point, date and drop details. We confirm your driver and fare within 15 minutes.

Are your fares fixed or metered?

Every local day tour, airport transfer and outstation trip is quoted as one fixed, all-inclusive fare before you confirm — so there's no meter running and no end-of-trip surprise.

Do you provide one-day sightseeing packages for visitors?

Yes. Our tour packages page lists curated half-day and full-day itineraries, each with a fixed stop list, waiting time and a driver familiar with the route.

Which areas do you operate in?

Shreyas Tours and Travels is based in Pune and covers the Pune Metropolitan Region, plus outstation routes to Lonavala, Mahabaleshwar, Nashik, Mumbai and Goa.
