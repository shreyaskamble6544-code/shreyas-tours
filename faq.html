Cab booking and Pune tour questions

Shreyas Tours and Travels answers the practical questions customers ask about fares, vehicles, drivers, airport pickups, payments, and changes. Answers were reviewed on 16 August 2026.

What services does Shreyas Tours and Travels provide?

Shreyas Tours and Travels provides local Pune sightseeing tours, airport pickups and drops, one-way and round-trip outstation cabs, corporate employee transport, and multi-vehicle event travel. Each booking is matched to a vehicle category and trip scope before confirmation.

How do I book a cab with Shreyas Tours and Travels?

Submit the booking form or contact Shreyas Tours and Travels by telephone or WhatsApp. Share the pickup location, destination or itinerary, travel date, pickup time, and number of passengers. The team confirms availability, vehicle type, fare, and inclusions before accepting the booking.

Are the fares fixed or metered?

Local tours and airport transfers are quoted as fixed fares before confirmation. Outstation trips are quoted using the route, duration, vehicle category, and expected kilometre allowance. Check the written quote for tolls, parking, state taxes, waiting time, and driver allowance.

What is included in a tour package price?

A package quote identifies the vehicle category, trip duration, passenger limit, route or stop list, and included waiting time. Online prices are indicative starting fares. The final written quote should be checked for tolls, parking, entry tickets, extra kilometres, and overtime.

Does Shreyas Tours and Travels track airport flights?

Shreyas Tours and Travels monitors the flight details supplied for an airport pickup. Pickup coordination is adjusted when the arrival time changes. Airport bookings include 45 minutes of complimentary waiting time at arrivals; the confirmation states where that period begins and any later charge.

Which areas does Shreyas Tours and Travels serve?

Shreyas Tours and Travels primarily serves the Pune Metropolitan Region and operates outstation routes across Maharashtra. Common routes include Lonavala, Pune, Nashik, Alibaug, and Mahabaleshwar, with selected interstate trips subject to availability.

What vehicle types are available?

Available categories include hatchbacks, air-conditioned sedans, SUVs, and tempo travellers. Passenger count, luggage, trip distance, and accessibility needs determine the recommended option. The exact category is confirmed before dispatch.

Are the drivers verified?

Shreyas Tours and Travels states that its drivers are identity-verified and hold the licences required for the assigned commercial vehicle. Customers receive driver and vehicle details before pickup and should contact the booking team if the arriving details differ.

How can customers pay?

Cash and UPI are accepted for standard bookings. Corporate customers can request consolidated invoicing subject to an agreed account arrangement. The confirmation specifies whether an advance is required and when the balance is due.

What happens if I cancel or change a booking?

Cancellation and rescheduling terms depend on the trip type, notice period, and vehicle allocation. Customers should request the applicable terms in writing before confirming. Shreyas Tours and Travels states any charge before processing a change.
